In 2026 I produced *Iyuana*, a week of film and photography about the Sápara Nation of the Ecuadorian Amazon, in Long Island City. I curated *Herencia Viva*, painter Luis Medina’s exhibition of Ecuadorian magic realism, at three venues including Flushing Town Hall. And for the Ecuadorian American Cultural Center, where I was Creative & Community Coordinator, I planned *Ayazamana*, a night of live music and traditional dance. {{TODO: confirm your role on Ayazamana}}

Before that, I spent four years at Flywheel, a digital commerce agency, art-directing Amazon and Walmart storefronts and ads for more than twenty health, beauty and consumer brands.

I studied Communication Design at City Tech, CUNY.
